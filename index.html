Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is IPTV Pro and how does it work? +
IPTV Pro is a premium IPTV subscription service that streams live TV, sports, movies and series over your internet connection. After you choose a plan, we send login details (M3U URL or Xtream Codes) on WhatsApp. Paste them into any compatible IPTV player app on your device and start watching — no satellite dish or cable box required.
❓ Do you offer a free IPTV test? +
Yes — request a free 24-hour personal IPTV test before you buy. Tell us your device on WhatsApp. Ask us about the connections available for your test. The Restream trial is separate (12 hours) and is explained on the Restream page.
❓ Which devices and apps work with IPTV Pro? +
IPTV Pro works on Smart TV (Samsung, LG and others), Android TV, Amazon Firestick, Android phones and tablets, MAG boxes, Enigma2, Windows, macOS, iOS and most popular IPTV player apps. Not sure which app to use? See our player apps page or ask on WhatsApp — we'll recommend the best setup for your device.
❓ How much does IPTV Pro cost? +
We offer affordable monthly plans for all regions. The European package is billed in EUR only — select it in the pricing section. Longer plans cost less per month than paying one month at a time. Contact us on WhatsApp for custom pricing in your region.
❓ What channels and VOD content are included? +
Packages include live channels plus movies and series on demand. Mini Asian focuses on Pakistan, India, Bangladesh and Asian sports; European is tuned for UK and EU viewers; World covers international entertainment. The World Personal catalogue currently lists about 98,531 live TV channels, 89,275 movies and 15,873 series titles — those figures can change and do not apply to every package. Browse the personal channel list or start a free test. Restream uses a separate directory.
❓ How do I install IPTV on Firestick or Smart TV? +
Install a compatible IPTV player (such as IPTV Smarters, TiviMate or your TV's IPTV app), then paste the M3U or Xtream login we send on WhatsApp. Most Firestick and Smart TV setups take under ten minutes. Step-by-step guides are on our IPTV tutorial page, and support can walk you through it live if you get stuck.
❓ How can I become an IPTV reseller? +
Message us on WhatsApp to open an IPTV Pro reseller panel. You get wholesale credit pricing, a dashboard to create and renew customer lines, and help getting started. Many resellers begin with friends and family and grow from there. Full details are on the reseller panel page.
❓ Can European customers pay in different currencies? +
No. The European package runs on a separate premium server and is billed in EUR only. Select the European tab in pricing to see euro prices. It is recommended for UK and European customers who want local channels and stable EU routing.
❓ What is IPTV Restream, and how is the trial different? +
IPTV Restream is a wholesale connection pool for your own server or panel (Xtream API or M3U). It is not a household subscription. The Restream trial is 12 hours with up to 10 connections and no IP whitelist. Paid Restream needs your server public IP whitelisted. Compare sizes on the Restream packages page.
